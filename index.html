No meat, no fish, no fuss.

Kave opened on Goldhawk Road in 2021, by Shepherd's Bush Green. Big windows, leather tub chairs, plants on every shelf and a neon sign over the counter that says life happens, coffee helps.

Everything we serve is vegetarian. The burgers, hot dogs, wraps and salads are vegan and halal. Come in at 6am for an espresso and an almond croissant, or stay for lunch at the window.
